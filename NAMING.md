# 🌺 Naming & Cultural Attribution

This project is named **Pueo** (the endemic Hawaiian short-eared owl, pronounced *poo-eh-oh*).

In Hawaiian culture, the pueo is traditionally revered as an **ʻaumākua**—an ancestral guardian spirit that watches over, guides, and protects a home and its family. Furthermore, the word *pueo* historically links to the **ʻaho pueo**, the main structural cross-beams that physically hold a traditional house together.

## Why this name?
We chose this name with deep humility and respect for the Hawaiian language (`ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi`) and culture. This AI agent's architecture directly mirrors the protective, vigilant, and self-healing traits of the pueo. It serves as a persistent digital guardian, ensuring your home's automation infrastructure remains stable and resilient.

## Our Commitment
In the spirit of open source and out of respect for Native Hawaiian traditional knowledge principles, the maintainers commit that **Pueo itself will always be free and open-source**. We will never sell it, offer it as a paid product, or seek a trademark on the name "Pueo".

The code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE), which lets anyone use, modify, and redistribute it, including commercially. That license covers the code only, not the name.

## A Request About the Name
Out of respect for the cultural meaning of *pueo*, we ask that you:
* Give forks, derivatives, and commercial products built on this code a different name, and not market them as "Pueo".
* Do not seek a trademark on "Pueo" or brand a commercial service with it.
* Keep this NAMING.md file when you redistribute the code, so the attribution travels with it.

These are requests, not license terms. We hope you will honor them.

To learn more about the conservation of this endangered endemic bird, please visit the [Honolulu Zoo Society Pueo Profile](https://honoluluzoo.org).
